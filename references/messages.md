# Writing to landlords so it sounds like a person

Landlords get a lot of copy-paste inquiries and a growing number of AI-written ones. A message that reads like a real person texting gets answered first. Run every draft through this pass before it goes out.

## The pass
1. **Match the channel.** A text is 2 to 4 short sentences. An email can be a little longer, but never more than one short paragraph plus a sign-off.
2. **Lead with the place, not yourself.** "Hi! Is the 1BR on Maker Cove still available?" beats an introduction.
3. **One or two personal facts, max.** Pick the ones a landlord cares about: why you're in town, how long, the pet. Drop the rest.
4. **Sound spoken.** Use contractions (I'm, I'd, it's). Use plain words: "place" not "residence", "move in" not "occupancy". Don't say "I am writing to inquire", "I would be delighted", "Please do not hesitate", "Thank you for your consideration".
5. **No AI tells.** No em dashes, no bulleted lists, no "I hope this message finds you well", no three-part lists of adjectives, no perfectly parallel sentences. One small casual touch is fine ("Thanks so much!"). Forced slang or typos are not.
6. **One clear ask.** Usually "Is it still available, and could I come see it this week?"
7. **Contact last.** Name and number on the last line.
8. **Pet line.** Weight and breed in one breath ("I have a 9 lb Havanese, super chill") plus a photo if the channel allows it.
9. **True only.** Humanising changes the tone, never the facts. Don't invent reasons for moving, a job, references, or anything a reference check could contradict. Leave out what's private, but don't make things up.
10. **Read it aloud.** If it sounds like a cover letter, cut it in half.

## Example
Stiff:
> Hello, I am writing to inquire about the availability of your one-bedroom apartment. I am a student at a local institution and I would be delighted to be considered as a tenant. Please do not hesitate to contact me.

Natural:
> Hi! Is the 1BR on Maker Cove still available? I just moved up from NYC for a 9-month furniture program in Rockport, so I'm in the shop most days and mostly need a quiet place to sleep. I have a 9 lb Havanese, super chill. Could I come see it this week? Thanks so much! Sam, 555-123-4567
